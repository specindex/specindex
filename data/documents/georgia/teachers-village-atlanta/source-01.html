Atlanta’s 33-Story Affordable Housing Tower Secures $77M Building Permit, Signaling Vertical Construction

Construction on Atlanta’s long-awaited Teachers Village project is finally poised to begin after months of setbacks. On March 19, 2026, the City of Atlanta issued a building permit valued at $77.125 million for a new

-story,
-square-foot multifamily residential rental building in the Fairlie-Poplar Historic District. The permit, filed jointly by developer

, comes just weeks after an October 2025 report revealed yet another delay that pushed the groundbreaking from October 2025 into the first quarter of 2026. With the major approval now in hand, the project appears set to move from planning to active construction in the coming weeks.

Backed by public funding from Invest Atlanta, the intergenerational community near Centennial Olympic Park aims to provide deeply affordable homes for essential workers and seniors while injecting new residential and commercial energy into downtown.
