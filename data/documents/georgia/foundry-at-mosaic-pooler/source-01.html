Thompson Thrift Breaks Ground on 300-Unit Apartment Community Near Savannah

July 20, 2026

Thompson Thrift has begun development of The Foundry at Mosaic, a 300-unit luxury apartment community in Pooler, Georgia, expanding the company’s multifamily presence in the fast-growing Savannah market.

Construction is expected to begin this month, with the first residents anticipated to move in during November 2027.

“The Foundry at Mosaic represents another exciting opportunity to expand our presence in one of the Southeast’s most dynamic growth markets,” said Josh Purvis, managing partner for Thompson Thrift Residential. “Pooler continues to benefit from exceptional population growth, major employment investments and strong housing demand.”

Located within Mosaic Town Center at the intersection of Pooler Parkway and Interstate 16, the community will offer one-, two- and three-bedroom apartments with floor plans of up to 1,475 square feet. Units will feature granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, smart-home technology, full-size washers and dryers, walk-in closets and private outdoor living options.

Amenities will include a resort-style swimming pool, fitness center, pickleball court, golf simulator, dog park, conference room, outdoor entertainment areas and multiple indoor and outdoor gathering spaces.

The development is Thompson Thrift’s second multifamily project near Savannah Quarters. The company recently completed The Liliana, a 360-unit luxury apartment community in Pooler that is currently leasing.

The Foundry at Mosaic will be financed with equity from the Thompson Thrift 2025 Multifamily Development LP.

Thompson Thrift is a full-service real estate company engaged in the development, construction, leasing and management of multifamily and commercial properties nationwide. Founded in 1986, the company operates from offices in Indiana, Colorado, Texas and Arizona.
