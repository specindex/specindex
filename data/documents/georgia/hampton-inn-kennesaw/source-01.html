Vector Hospitality Inc. Breaks Ground on Hampton Inn by Hilton Atlanta NW Kennesaw/Town Center

Jun 3, 2026 3 minute read

Summary

Vector Hospitality breaks ground on a 123-room Hampton Inn in Kennesaw’s Town Center corridor off Interstate 75, scheduled to open in Summer 2027. The hotel caters to metro Atlanta’s fastest-growing suburban market with amenities such as meeting space and fitness facilities while supporting local growth and creating jobs during construction and operations.

KENNESAW, GA. – June 2, 2026 – Vector Hospitality, Inc., an Atlanta-based hotel developer, is proud to announce the groundbreaking of the 123-room Hampton Inn Atlanta Kennesaw NW/Town Center, located off Cobb Place Lane NW within the thriving Town Center corridor in the heart of Cobb County. The project represents another meaningful milestone in Vector Hospitality’s continued investment throughout the Metro Atlanta market and reflects the company’s long-standing commitment to developing hospitality experiences that thoughtfully serve growing communities.

Scheduled to open in Summer 2027, the Hampton Inn Atlanta Kennesaw NW/Town Center will bring a fresh, modern hospitality offering to one of metro Atlanta’s fastest-growing suburban business and leisure markets. Ideally positioned near Interstate 75, the hotel will provide convenient access to Kennesaw State University, Town Center at Cobb, Truist Park, Midtown and Downtown Atlanta, and numerous corporate offices, retail destinations, and entertainment venues throughout Cobb County and the greater Atlanta region.

Designed with today’s traveler in mind, the hotel will blend Hampton by Hilton’s signature warmth and reliability with contemporary comfort and functional design. Guests will enjoy thoughtfully appointed guestrooms, complimentary hot breakfast, flexible meeting space, a modern fitness center, outdoor gathering areas, and Hilton’s signature Hamptonality service experience.

“Breaking ground on this project is especially meaningful for our team as Kennesaw and Cobb County have been an important part of our hospitality roots for nearly two decades,” said Aman Patel, President of Vector Hospitality. “We continue to see tremendous long-term growth throughout this corridor, driven by expanding business activity, tourism, and the area’s connectivity to major Atlanta destinations. We are excited to bring another Hilton product to this dynamic and evolving market while continuing to invest in the community’s future.”

The project arrives at a time of continued growth throughout the Kennesaw and Town Center markets, fueled by expanding corporate presence, residential development, higher education demand, and increased tourism throughout northwest metro Atlanta. The Hampton Inn Atlanta NW Kennesaw/Town Center is expected to support this momentum while generating new jobs during both construction and ongoing hotel operations.

The Hampton Inn Atlanta Kennesaw NW/Town Center is more than a hotel development—it is an investment in the continued evolution of one of metro Atlanta’s most active commercial corridors. As the surrounding market continues to expand, the hotel is positioned to serve as a welcoming destination for business travelers, families, university visitors, and guests exploring the greater Atlanta area.
