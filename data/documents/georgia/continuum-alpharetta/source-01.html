Continuum Alpharetta

Alpharetta, Georgia 30004 - View City Profile

Virtual Tour

Description

Continuum Alpharetta is a 52-acre mixed-use development in the highly sought-after city of Alpharetta, Georgia. Fully entitled and under active development, the project includes a renovated 515,438 RSF office building at 5555 Windward Parkway, completed in August 2025. An additional 808,000 SF of future office space is available for build-to-suit opportunities. Over 250,000 SF of office space is currently available for lease, including 75,000 SF of upgraded second-generation space with furniture. 

The retail district will span 74,000 SF, with the first phase approximately 20,000 SF integrated into the luxury multifamily component and scheduled to break ground in 2026. Residential offerings include 280 luxury apartments and 98 upscale townhomes, both slated to begin construction in 2026. 

A large portion of the site will be preserved as greenspace, featuring interior walking trails. Infrastructure upgrades include improvements to roads, underground utilities, parking areas, landscaping, hardscape elements, and signage/wayfinding. 

Blurring the line between 'return to work' and 'workplace retreat', Continuum boldly envisions North Fulton's response to how work works in an ever-changing climate. 

Located at the corner of Windward Parkway and GA-400, it offers unmatched brand visibility and direct access to Avalon, major employment centers, and residential neighborhoods, making it the ideal headquarters for companies seeking dynamic design, elevated lifestyle integration, and seamless connectivity.
