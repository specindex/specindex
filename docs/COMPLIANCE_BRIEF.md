## 1. Purpose and Status

This brief governs development and operation of SpecIndex while the founder remains subject to post-employment obligations owed to ConstructConnect. It is an operational control document: every collaborator, contractor, development assistant, and product workflow should follow it.

Current legal posture: High enforcement risk. Georgia law applies. The founder signed the separation agreement, received no equity grants, and serves as SpecIndex's CEO. The principal restrictive period is assumed to run from June 15, 2026 through June 15, 2027, subject to any contractual tolling argument.

## 2. Core Rules

- Use public and independently developed information only.
- Do not use, request, reconstruct, summarize, or infer ConstructConnect confidential information.
- Do not import ConstructConnect files, datasets, customer lists, pricing, source code, product roadmaps, internal analyses, credentials, or unpublished business information.
- Do not solicit restricted ConstructConnect customers, prospects, employees, vendors, or partners.
- Do not describe SpecIndex as a ConstructConnect replacement, counter-product, or strategy derived from inside knowledge.
- Maintain contemporaneous evidence of independent creation and lawful data provenance.
- Escalate uncertainty before acting; do not assume that public availability of raw data permits use of confidential methods or conclusions.

## 3. Product Perimeter

**Permitted Development Direction**

- Manufacturer-focused specification and brand intelligence.
- Public permit, procurement, bid, announcement, and specification sources obtained lawfully.
- Independent product design based on public market research and direct customer discovery outside restricted relationships.
- Source-linked project records, public citations, manufacturer workflows, and independently designed user experiences.
- General infrastructure, security, authentication, billing, public-data ingestion, and quality-control systems developed without former-employer materials.

**Activities Requiring Legal Clearance**

- Commercial launch or paid sales before June 15, 2027.
- Contractor-facing project-lead products or features closely resembling ConstructConnect offerings.
- Outreach to any organization with which the founder had material dealings or about which the founder received confidential information.
- Features based on remembered internal roadmaps, customer complaints, pricing, performance metrics, product gaps, or strategic priorities.
- Hiring, contracting with, or soliciting current or recent ConstructConnect personnel.
- Competitive comparison content naming ConstructConnect or asserting nonpublic product limitations.
- Patent filings or ownership representations covering concepts potentially conceived during employment.

## 4. Data Clean-Room Protocol

- Record the exact public URL, access date, source owner, license or access terms, and retrieval method for each source.
- Store raw public-source material separately from processed datasets and preserve immutable retrieval logs.
- Reject any source supplied from a former-employer system, account, export, email, device, or private communication.
- Do not recreate nonpublic datasets or reports from memory.
- Document transformation logic, extraction prompts, code commits, and validation methods.
- Require every contributor to certify that no third-party confidential information was contributed.
- Quarantine questionable material immediately and do not delete it; preserve it for legal review.

## 5. IP Provenance Record

Maintain a dated chronology and supporting evidence for:

- First conception of each material feature or business method.
- First written description, prototype, code commit, and production deployment.
- Domain registration, incorporation, funding, and customer-contact dates.
- Devices, accounts, repositories, personnel, and working time used.
- Public sources and independent market research supporting the feature.
- Any pre-June 15, 2026 activity, which must be identified accurately and escalated.

Never backdate, overwrite, delete, or manufacture provenance evidence. Preserve version histories and original documents even when prospective language is corrected.

## 6. Restricted Relationships

Maintain a confidential restricted-party register covering ConstructConnect customers, prospects, employees, vendors, distributors, and other key relationships with whom the founder had material contact, supervised communications, or received confidential information during the contractual lookback period.

- Screen outreach lists before contact.
- Do not evade restrictions through intermediaries, contractors, automated campaigns, or introductions.
- General untargeted advertising is distinct from targeted solicitation, but campaigns must not be designed to reach restricted parties specifically.
- Document why each material prospect is outside the restricted category before targeted engagement.

## 7. Communications and Marketing Rules

**Do Not Say**

- The idea came from ConstructConnect.
- SpecIndex is the answer to, counter-strategy for, or replacement for ConstructConnect.
- The founder knows what ConstructConnect cannot do, plans to do, charges, or prioritizes based on internal experience.
- SpecIndex exploits gaps learned through confidential customer, product, operational, or financial information.

**Approved Framing**

- SpecIndex is independently built from public records and independently conducted market research.
- The product serves building-product manufacturers and representative agencies through public-source specification intelligence.
- Every material project fact is linked to a lawful public source.
- SpecIndex maintains controls against use of third-party confidential information.

## 9. Escalation Triggers

- A demand letter, preservation notice, subpoena, lawsuit, or contact from ConstructConnect or its counsel.
- A customer, investor, employee, or collaborator asks about competitive overlap or former-employer information.
- Discovery of former-employer information on any personal or company system.
- A proposed customer or hire may be within a restricted category.
- A feature appears connected to work, concepts, or anticipated development from the employment period.
- A contemplated public statement names ConstructConnect or compares capabilities.
- Any request to delete, conceal, rewrite, or backdate potentially relevant evidence.

## 10. Negotiated Resolution Objective

The preferred resolution is a written, non-admission waiver or carve-out confirming that ConstructConnect will not object to SpecIndex operating within an agreed perimeter: public data only; no confidential information; no solicitation of covered customers or employees; manufacturer-focused workflows; and specified limitations on overlapping offerings through June 15, 2027.

No informal outreach should be made before the factual chronology, clean-room audit, restricted-party register, and product-differentiation analysis are complete.

## 12. Working Instruction

When uncertain, preserve the material, pause the activity, and escalate. The objective is not merely to avoid copying files; it is to demonstrate independent creation, lawful sourcing, non-solicitation, and a product strategy that does not depend on confidential former-employer knowledge.
